Football — records that still stand

The Record Sheet

A short list of numbers the game hasn't matched yet — some decades old, some still being written.

91goals

Most goals in a calendar year

Lionel Messi scored 91 goals for club and country in 2012, a single-year tally no player has come close to since.

Most Ballon d'Or wins

Messi holds eight Ballon d'Or awards, well clear of any other player in the trophy's history.

Longest unbeaten run, top-flight club

Steaua București went unbeaten in domestic league play from 1986 to 1989 — 104 straight matches.

Longest unbeaten Premier League run

Arsenal's "Invincibles" squad went unbeaten across 49 consecutive league matches from 2003 to 2004.

Most Champions League/European Cup wins by a club

Real Madrid's collection stands well ahead of the field, including five straight titles from 1956 to 1960.
